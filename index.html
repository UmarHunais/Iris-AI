About IRIS AI

Transforming Retinal Screening Through Intelligent Technology

IRIS AI is an AI-assisted retinal screening platform developed to support healthcare professionals in identifying potential signs of diabetic retinopathy from retinal fundus images.

The system supports efficient image submission, automated analysis, and a clear presentation of screening results.
